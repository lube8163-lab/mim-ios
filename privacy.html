Privacy Policy for Mim

Mim is a beta social application that allows users to share images and interact with generated content.

Information We Collect

  • Account information provided by the user (such as username and email address)
  • Content uploaded by the user (images and related metadata)
  • Basic usage data for app functionality and debugging

How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate the core features of the app
  • To improve stability and user experience
  • To communicate with users regarding the app, if necessary

Data Storage and Security

Data is transmitted using standard secure communication protocols (HTTPS). Image analysis and generation processes are performed locally on the user’s device whenever possible.

Third-Party Services

Cloud infrastructure services may be used to store and process data. Apple-provided system services are used where applicable.

Tracking and Advertising

Mim does not track users across apps or websites. Mim does not display third-party advertisements or sell user data.

User Control

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated during the beta period.
